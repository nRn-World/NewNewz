Privacy Policy and Disclaimer

Applies to the NewNewz website (nrnworld.one/NewNewz), the installable web app and the Android app · Last updated 12 November 2026

In short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics and we do not sell data.
  • Your favourites, saved stories and settings stay on your device.
  • To show news about a place, the app asks public news and community services about that place. Those services see the request and your IP address.
  • Notifications and location are optional.
  • NewNewz does not write, check or endorse the news and forum posts it shows. Some of it may be false, misleading, outdated or upsetting. You use the app at your own risk (see section 10).

1Who we are

NewNewz is a free app published by nRn World, a Swedish developer. It shows local news and forum discussions for any country, city, neighbourhood or street. For questions about this policy, see section 11.

2What stays on your device

Your favourite places and their alert settings, saved stories, chosen language, quiet hours and cached headlines and translations are stored on your device, in browser storage or in the Android app. We cannot see this data. You can delete it by clearing the app's data in your browser or device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

3Services that receive requests

NewNewz does not have its own news database. When you open a place, the app asks the services below about it. They receive the place name or coordinates you chose, and, as with any website, your IP address and device type. Each service has its own privacy policy, and we do not control it.

4Location

The app only uses your device location if you tap the locate button. Your coordinates are then sent to Photon to find the name of the place where you are. We do not store them. You can deny location access in your browser or device settings, and the rest of the app works without it.

5Notifications

Notifications are optional. If you turn them on:

  • Website and installed web app: we store your push subscription, language, time zone, quiet hours and your list of favourite places (names, coordinates and chosen topics) on our Cloudflare account, so that we can look for news and send you an alert.
  • Android app: the checks for your favourite places run on your device.

You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

6Retention and deletion

Notification data on our servers is deleted automatically after 90 days without use, and immediately when you remove all your favourite places or turn notifications off. Data on your device stays until you remove it. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies.

7Children

NewNewz shows real news, including reports about crime, accidents and violence. It is not intended for children.

8Your rights

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data we hold about you, to object to its processing and to withdraw consent at any time. We process notification data on the basis of your consent, and request data needed to show you news on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating the app. You can complain to your data protection authority, in Sweden the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY). To use your rights, contact us as described in section 11.

9Security

Connections between the app and our relays use HTTPS. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of data sent to third-party services.

10Content disclaimer and use at your own risk

Please read this section. NewNewz is a tool that finds and displays content published by others. We do not write, edit, verify, moderate or endorse that content, and we do not stand behind it. Everything you do with the app, and everything you decide based on what it shows, is at your own responsibility.

We do not vouch for the content

Headlines, summaries, forum posts and police notices come from third parties and are collected automatically. They can be false, misleading, incomplete, out of date, exaggerated or satirical. For example:

  • A headline may report a fire, an arrest or a threat on a street that turns out not to exist, or that is in a different city with the same name.
  • A forum post may repeat a rumour, a conspiracy theory or an unproven accusation, and a person named in a crime report may later be cleared.
  • A story may be old news that is being shared again, or may be written to provoke.
  • Machine translation can change the meaning of a sentence or reverse it.
  • A story can be matched to the wrong place because the place name is common.

Opinions in forums and comments are those of their authors, not ours. Items marked as police come from the police sources named in the app, and they are the authority's words, not ours.

Content that may upset you

The news about a place can include crime, violence, accidents, disasters, death and other distressing subjects. We do not choose which stories appear. You can use the topic filters, avoid opening stories you do not want to read, or stop using the app if it affects your well-being. If you feel unwell after reading something, talk to someone you trust or contact a health professional.

Not for emergencies, safety or advice

NewNewz is not an alert, warning or emergency service. News can be delayed or missing, so never rely on the app to find out whether you or someone else is in danger. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (for example 112 in the EU). Nothing in the app is legal, medical, financial, safety or travel advice, and you should not use it as the only basis for decisions about where to live, travel or invest.

No warranty and limited liability

The app is provided "as is" and "as available", without promises that it will be accurate, complete, uninterrupted or free of errors. To the extent the law allows, nRn World is not liable for any loss, damage, distress or cost that arises from using the app or from content shown in it, including content you reach through links. Nothing in this section limits liability that cannot be limited by law, and it does not affect your rights as a consumer.

Links and third-party sites

Opening a story takes you to a site that we do not control. You do so at your own risk, and that site's own terms and privacy policy apply. NewNewz is not affiliated with, and is not endorsed by, the news organisations, police authorities, forums and other services it mentions. Their names belong to their owners.

If you disagree with a story

We cannot change or remove what other publishers have written. If a story is wrong, harmful or unlawful, contact the original publisher or platform. If the app shows something clearly illegal, tell us (section 11) and we will look at whether the source should be blocked.
